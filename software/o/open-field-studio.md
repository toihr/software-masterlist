## Overview
A free app for construction inspections, energy label surveys, monument inspections and handovers, focused on the Netherlands. It works offline.

## Key Features
- Defect tickets on floor plans with priorities, status workflow and assignment
- 15 built-in checklist templates including NEN 2767 condition scoring
- Handover workflow with certificate and signatures; photo capture with GPS
- IFC viewer; export to PDF, HTML, BCF 2.1 and JSON
- Connectors: ERPNext, n8n/webhook, AFAS, Exact Online, Bouw7, Woningborg WKI, SWK, KYP

## Limitations
- Early stage (v0.x); no published releases seen on the repo page
- Registry and connector integrations are Dutch-focused
- Two-way import only for ERPNext and n8n so far
- No macOS or iOS build mentioned
- Does an update check against GitHub; account and data handling are not documented
