## Overview
A speech-to-text app that runs entirely on your computer and is presented as a local alternative to Wispr Flow.

## Key Features
- Dictate into any application with a global hotkey (Ctrl+Shift+Space)
- Whisper models from 75 MB to 3.1 GB on GPU (CUDA/Vulkan) or CPU
- 99+ languages with automatic detection; custom dictionary
- Meeting recording of microphone and PC audio

## Limitations
- Meeting capture is Windows-only; the page does not list other platforms explicitly
- Larger models need a GPU
- Models are downloaded separately in the app
- CUDA support needs a build feature flag
- Telemetry and account requirements are not documented
