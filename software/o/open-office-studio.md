## Overview
An office suite from the OpenAEC Foundation that runs entirely on your computer, with no account or cloud service. It works directly on Microsoft Office file formats.

## Key Features
- Writer, spreadsheet and presentation modules for DOCX, XLSX and PPTX, with track changes and comments
- Opens Access MDB/ACCDB as an editable SQLite copy; sandboxed VBA
- Notes module (free canvas or Markdown) and IMAP/SMTP email client
- Spell checking in Dutch and English, PDF export, crash recovery

## Limitations
- Pre-release; the authors advise testing on copies of files
- ODF, DOC/XLS/PPT and RTF need a separately installed LibreOffice
- Access form designs and macros are inventoried but not executed
- Code, commits and most documentation are in Dutch
- Project page is inconsistent about whether the app checks for updates

## Compared to Alternatives
- **LibreOffice:** far more mature and complete; this focuses on Office formats and is much younger.
